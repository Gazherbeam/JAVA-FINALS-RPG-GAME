# StationSupply Pixel RPG UI: Free Sample

A handful of pieces and 10 icons from the Pixel RPG UI packs, in the Classic Wood theme.

## What is inside

- `ui/wood/<1x|2x|4x>/`: wide and square buttons in five states, two switches (normal, disabled), the item slot in four states, window panel, long ribbon, title plate, bar frame, bar trough, red and gold bar fills, one star.
- `icons/` (colour) and `icons_white/` (single-colour white): 10 icons: dagger, gold ring, steel helmet, blue potion, red potion, gold key, ruby, chest, gear, home. Canvases are 64x64 px.
- `nine_slice.json` for the pieces in this sample, `preview_wood.png`, `fonts/`, `LICENSE.txt`.

1x files are the art as drawn; 2x and 4x are exact nearest-neighbour copies. In Godot set the default texture filter to Nearest; in Unity use Point (no filter). State files are padded by 1 transparent pixel on every side (see `nine_slice.json`).

## Want the rest?

The full pack has six colour themes, 213 icons in colour and 189 in white, 12 avatar crests, 20 ready-made screens and a Godot 4 demo: see the StationSupply itch.io page
